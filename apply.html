What kind of work comes up?+

Projects can include rack and stack, equipment installation and swaps, decommissioning, copper and fiber cabling, camera and access-control systems, fire alarm work, and electrical work. Tell us about other field skills you bring, too.

Can I apply with a trade license or specialty?+

Yes. List the trade or systems you work on and where you’re licensed or certified, if applicable. We match project requirements to the people qualified to do the work.

How pay and travel work+

Pay and any travel arrangements are discussed for each assignment before you decide whether to accept.

How we use your information+

Roddy Field Services LLC keeps your contact details, skills, home base, and requested pay. If you opt in, we text that mobile number about matching jobs, start times, and site details. Message frequency varies (typically a few messages per matching job, usually under 10 per month). Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out. Reply HELP for help. Consent to texts is not a condition of applying. Mobile numbers and SMS opt-in data are not shared with third parties for marketing. We ask before submitting your name to a customer.
