THE ROLE

Project-based
field technician.

What the work is+

Rack-and-stack and equipment swaps on existing power. Copper and fiber support, patching, and labeling. De-rack, pack, and stage listed gear. Site surveys, serials, photos, and closeout notes. Travel to other sites when the project includes it.

What this is not+

New 120 V circuits, hardwired UPS, fire alarm, cameras, or access control. Licensed electrical work stays with a licensed electrician. We will tell you the scope before you accept a project.

How pay and travel work+

Pay is set per project, typically $28–$42/hour. You review the rate, dates, and site before you accept. Overnight work includes lodging and per diem when the customer funds them. Drive time and overtime are written into the assignment.

How we use your information+

We keep your contact details, skills, home base, and requested pay. We text and call the mobile number you give us about matching jobs, start times, and site details. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to a text to opt out. We do not sell the list. We ask before submitting your name to a customer.
