Project questions

What should we send for a quote?+

Send the site location, equipment list or photos, quantities, dates, and what you need done. Include access restrictions and any required testing or documentation.

Can you work with our technical team?+

Yes. We handle the agreed physical work and coordinate with your technical lead, who owns configuration and final system checks. Share any on-site assistance you need during startup.

Can you take on other on-site work?+

Send the details. Related work can include a site survey, an equipment inventory, or a dealer or integrator’s rollout. We’ll confirm what can be included in the quote.

What about electrical disconnects and disposal?+

We’ll agree on who handles licensed electrical disconnects, certified data destruction, and specialized disposal before work begins. Those tasks require appropriately qualified providers.
