PRIVACY POLICY · NOVEMBER 15, 2026

How we use your information.

This policy is for Roddy Field Services LLC, a Wyoming limited liability company. Trade name: Roddy Field Services. Contact: James@roddyfs.com · +1 (307) 212-2648 · Rock Springs, Wyoming.


Job texts and technician applications

The technician application at apply.roddyfs.com posts to our server. We email James@roddyfs.com and keep your message, resume, and cover letter so we can reply about work.

If you check the SMS box, Roddy Field Services LLC may send job-related text messages to the mobile number you provide about available work, dispatch, schedules, start times, and site details. This is not a marketing list. Consent to texts is not a condition of applying. You can submit the form without agreeing to texts; we will still email you.

Message frequency varies. Typically a few messages per matching job, usually under 10 per month. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to a job text to opt out. Reply HELP for help. You can also email James@roddyfs.com or call +1 (307) 212-2648.

Mobile numbers and SMS opt-in data and consent will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. We do not sell applicant lists. We ask before submitting your name to a customer. We keep application records, including phone numbers and whether you opted in to texts, while you are on the bench and for up to 24 months after the last contact, then delete or archive them unless we still need them for a project or legal record.


What our visitor counter records

We count visits to the home page, anonymous browser-tab sessions, device-size categories, referring website domains, and clicks on email, phone, staffing, and prepared-inquiry controls. A randomly generated session identifier stays in your browser tab’s session storage. The counter does not store your name, email address, full browsing history, IP address, page-query strings, or the contents of our project form.


How we use website counts

Roddy Field Services LLC uses these counts to understand website interest and improve its services. Raw analytics events are retained for up to 90 days. Short-lived keyed network hashes help limit abusive traffic. Our hosting providers necessarily process network requests to deliver the website. The site also loads fonts from Google Fonts.


Your choices

Our counter respects Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control. You can also turn off this counter on this browser. This preference is saved in local storage; clearing browser storage clears the preference.




Project inquiries

The project form posts to our server. We email James@roddyfs.com and keep the message so we can reply about work. Analytics does not receive those form contents. Contact us at James@roddyfs.com with questions about information you have provided.
