Simple traffic counts.

What our visitor counter records

We count visits to the home page, anonymous browser-tab sessions, device-size categories, referring website domains, and clicks on email, phone, staffing, and prepared-inquiry controls. A randomly generated session identifier stays in your browser tab’s session storage. The counter does not store your name, email address, full browsing history, IP address, page-query strings, or the contents of our project form.


How we use the information

Roddy Field Services uses these counts to understand website interest and improve its services. Raw analytics events are retained for up to 90 days. Short-lived keyed network hashes help limit abusive traffic. Our hosting providers necessarily process network requests to deliver the website. The site also loads fonts from Google Fonts.


Your choices

Our counter respects Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control. You can also turn off this counter on this browser. This preference is saved in local storage; clearing browser storage clears the preference.
